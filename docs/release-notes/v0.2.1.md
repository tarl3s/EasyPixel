## EasyPixel v0.2.1

**Open a drawing inside the canvas you're working on**
When you open a file (File → Open or Recent), EasyPixel asks how: as its own sprite (like before) or **placed in this canvas**. Placed, the drawing comes in floating, ready to move; press Enter to drop it. Handy to move a 32×32 drawing into a new 64×64 sprite.

**Resize what you selected by dragging**
With any selection tool (or Move), eight small handles appear around the selection. Drag one to make the pixels bigger or smaller — corners change both sides, edges change one. Hold **Shift** to keep the proportions, **Esc** while dragging to go back. Pixels stay sharp, and going big and then small again loses nothing. Enter drops them; one Ctrl+Z undoes it.

**Animations are saved in their own folder**
Saving a sprite with more than one frame now creates a folder named after it, with the editable `.epx` and every frame as a PNG (`walk_001.png`, `walk_002.png`…). Each save keeps the PNGs up to date.
