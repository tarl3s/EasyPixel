## EasyPixel v0.3.2

**New tools**
- **Jumble (⁂)**: rub over your drawing and the pixels swap places with their neighbours, like LibreSprite's tool.
  Great for grass, dirt, stone and noisy edges. No colour is added or lost.
- **Blur (≈)**: softens what you paint over; each stroke blurs a little more. **Strength** sets how much.

**One window**
Double-clicking an `.epx` (or any picture) while EasyPixel is open now opens it as a new tab in that window,
instead of a new window each time.

**Flip in sight**
With a selection tool or Move, the top bar has **Flip horizontally**, **Flip vertically** and 90° turns for the
selected area.

**New icon**
A green pixel-art pencil on a dark tile, in the same style as EasyEngine.

**Fix**
`.epx` files made by other tools that wrote their palette colours in a different order now open with the right
colours (red and blue were swapped).
