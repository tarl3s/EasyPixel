## EasyPixel v0.2.0 — Learn pixel art

Never drawn pixel art before? EasyPixel can now teach you. Open **Help → Learn pixel art**.

**18 lessons, from zero to game-ready**

- Start with pixels and canvas sizes, clean lines and curves, and readable shapes.
- Learn colour palettes, light and shading, outlines, anti-aliasing, dithering and textures.
- Animate: the basics, a walk cycle, and character sprites.
- Build a game world: tilesets, backgrounds with parallax, buildings and props, items and icons, and effects like fire, smoke and explosions.
- Finish with getting your sprites ready for a game (and into EasyEngine).

Every lesson has pictures drawn by EasyPixel itself, with good and bad examples side by side, tips and common mistakes.

**Practise right away**

- Each lesson ends with **Try it in the editor**: a practice canvas opens with a faint guide and a lesson bar on top.
- Draw, then press **Check**. EasyPixel tells you what is right and points at what to fix, pixel by pixel.
- Tick the few things only you can judge, and the lesson gets a ★ in the list.
- Save your practice and come back later: open the file again and the lesson bar returns, ready to check.

**Languages:** the lessons are written in English and Português (Brasil). In the other languages the editor stays translated and the lessons show in English.

Windows may warn that the app isn't signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.
