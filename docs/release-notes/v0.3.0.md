## EasyPixel v0.3.0

**Create a sprite by describing it**
Choose **File → ✨ Create with AI…** and say what you want, in any language: *"a small red slime with big eyes"*.
EasyPixel shows four pixel-art options. Pick the one you like and it becomes a new sprite, or a new layer on the
sprite you have open. Before you generate, pick the size (16, 32, 48 or 64 pixels), the view and the palette.
**4 more** gives you four new options from the same words.

**Your drawing stays yours**
The picture comes back as real pixels, not a blurry image. Change it with any tool you already know: pencil, bucket,
selection, layers. Undo works as always.

**Free to start**
Open **Edit → AI settings…** and add a free Cloudflare key: make a free Cloudflare account, open Workers AI, copy your
Account ID and create an API token. That's enough for roughly 100 pictures a day for free (an estimate).

**Or use your own key**
OpenAI, Gemini and Retro Diffusion also work. Paste your key in AI settings. Those services charge you
directly; EasyPixel doesn't take a cut, and the keys stay on your computer.

Pollinations also works with a free key from its site, set in the same place.

**Several sprites at once: tabs**
Every sprite you open or create gets its own tab above the canvas, with its own undo. Switch with a click or
**Ctrl+Tab**, close with the ✕, the middle mouse button or **Ctrl+W**. Closing EasyPixel asks about each tab with
unsaved changes.

**Turn a selection by any angle**
Select something, move the mouse just outside a corner of the selection (the cursor becomes a hand) and drag around.
**Shift** turns in steps of 15°. Pixels stay crisp: no blur, no new colours. **Edit → Rotate by angle…** turns by an
exact number of degrees.

**Your palette, every time**
The last palette you load is remembered: new sprites (and the next time you open EasyPixel) start with it.
**⋯ → New sprites: back to the default palette** undoes that.

**More palette files**
Load palettes from Photoshop and Illustrator swatches (.ase, .aco, .act), Paint.NET (.txt), Windows .pal, and from any
picture or sprite: its colours become the palette (handy for palettes downloaded as PNG).

**Smaller things**
- The bucket tool's icon now looks like a bucket.
- In the lessons, **Check** shows what to fix first, so it's never hidden under the other results.
